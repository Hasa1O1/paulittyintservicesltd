About Paulitty

A foundation of service. A future in print.

Built on service. Driven by quality.

Paulitty International Services Limited is an independent product marketing and general trading company based in Kitwe on the Copperbelt of Zambia.

It started as a secretarial services company, then began printing, and today printing is our full-time business. Our primary objective is to build long-term, mutually beneficial relationships with customers by delivering and supporting high-quality services at competitive prices.

Paulitty was incorporated in 2009. Its directors are Mr. Paul Moses Musonda and Mrs. Litty Mayonda Musonda.
